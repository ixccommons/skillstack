# Research — [company or product name]

Every consequential item here also exists as a structured entry in
`pipeline-state.json` under `research[]`. This file is the readable version;
the state file is what `validate_pipeline.py` checks.

Every row needs: claim, value, provenance (PROVIDED, OBSERVED, SOURCED,
INFERENCE, ASSUMPTION, or UNKNOWN), a direct source URL when SOURCED,
retrieval date, geography, applicability, and confidence. See
`references/evidence-policy.md` for what each provenance type means and the
rules on benchmarks.
